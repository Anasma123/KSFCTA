The Kerala Self Financing College Teachers’ Association (KSFCTA) is an organisation established in 2023 for the welfare and collective interests of teachers working in self-financing colleges across Kerala.

The Association functions under the patronage of KPCTA and follows the democratic and progressive ideology of the Indian National Congress.

WHY JOIN KSFCTA?

If you are a teacher working in a self-financing college in Kerala, become a member of KSFCTA and be part of a collective platform working for the welfare, dignity, rights and professional interests of self-financing college teachers.

🤝

Strengthen Unity

Strengthen unity among self-financing college teachers across every institution in Kerala.

📢

Collective Platform

Raise teachers’ concerns effectively through a strong, unified collective platform.

⚖️

Welfare & Rights

Work for the welfare, dignity, and legitimate interests of self-financing college teachers.

📈

Professional Growth

Promote continuous professional development, academic excellence, and career growth.

🏛️

Stronger Community

Build a stronger, united teachers’ community across all 14 districts in Kerala.
