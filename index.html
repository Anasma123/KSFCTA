Who We Are

About KSCTA

Kerala Selfinancing College Teachers Association (KSCTA) is the premier organization representing teachers across hundreds of self-financing colleges in Kerala. We stand as a unified voice for the rights, dignity, and welfare of thousands of dedicated educators.

Self-financing college teachers form the backbone of higher education in Kerala, yet they often face challenges in pay parity, job security, and professional growth. KSCTA exists to change that — through solidarity, advocacy, and collective action.

  • ✓ Fighting for equal pay and service conditions for all teachers
  • ✓ Legal aid and support for members facing unfair treatment
  • ✓ Professional development and training opportunities
  • ✓ Strong representation before the government and UGC
  • ✓ Network of thousands of dedicated educators statewide
